Design notes (not part of the mockup itself):
· Pinned items (⭐) are the ones most apply-forms ask for first — GitHub, LinkedIn, resume. Could optionally surface pinned items on the home screen widget, same pattern as Progress's home summary.
· "Copy everything as text" builds one block like:
  Name · email · phone · github.com/... · linkedin.com/in/... · sanattudu.tech
  — for forms with a single "paste your info" box, or quick pasting into an email.
· Sections are open-ended like Progress Tracker's categories — "Profiles" / "Deployed Applications" / "Documents" / "Contact" ship as defaults, but nothing stops a "Visa Status" or "References" section.
· Deployed apps carry an optional stack-tag line (e.g. "Next.js · Express · Postgres") — handy context when a form or recruiter asks what you built it with.
· Documents reuse the existing MinIO bucket + upload pattern from the avatar feature, not a new storage mechanism.
· Read vs. Edit mode — try the "Edit layout" toggle at the top. Read mode (default) is a clean, fast copy surface — just Copy/Open/Pin, no clutter. Edit mode reveals rename/delete on every section (✎ / 🗑 next to each title) and edit/delete on every item, plus "add a field" / "add a section" rows — full CRUD on both sections and the topics/items inside them, never locked to the 4 defaults shown here.
